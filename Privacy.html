Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 24, 2026

Simply Amri ("the app") is a personal blood sugar tracking tool. This policy explains exactly what data the app collects, where it goes, and what stays only on your device.

The short version

Your glucose readings and HbA1c data never leave your device. They're stored locally in your browser's storage and are never sent to us, to any server, or to any third party. If you clear your browser data or uninstall the app, that data is gone — we don't have a copy.
The only information we ever receive is an email address, and only if you choose to type one into the "Join the Waitlist" field to hear about the Amri Wellness Hub. If you never use that field, we receive nothing about you at all.

What we collect

We collect exactly one thing, and only if you provide it yourself:

  • Email address — only if you voluntarily enter it into the waitlist signup field. This is sent to our email service provider, Beehiiv, so we can follow up with you about the Amri Wellness Hub coaching program. Along with your email, we send a short label describing why you signed up (for example, that you completed a 3-day logging streak), so our messages can be relevant instead of generic.

We do not collect your name, location, device identifiers, or any analytics about how you use the app, beyond what's described above.

What stays on your device

Everything else the app handles is stored only in your browser's local storage, on your own device:

  • Blood sugar readings, including the value, timing, and any notes you add
  • HbA1c baseline and follow-up values and dates
  • Your target range settings
  • Your first name, if you provide one when the app first asks

None of this is transmitted to us, stored on any server we control, or accessible to anyone but you. It exists only on the device where you entered it.

Third parties

If you sign up for the waitlist, your email address is stored and processed by Beehiiv, our email newsletter provider, so we can send you updates about the Amri Wellness Hub. You can review Beehiiv's own privacy practices at their website, and you can unsubscribe from our emails at any time using the link in any email we send.

We do not sell, rent, or share your information with advertisers, data brokers, or any other third party.

Your choices

  • To remove your local data — clear your browser's site data for Simply Amri, or uninstall the app. This deletes all your readings and settings permanently; we have no copy to restore.
  • To stop receiving emails — click "unsubscribe" in any email from us, or simply never enter your email into the waitlist field in the first place.

Children's privacy

Simply Amri is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Not a medical device

Simply Amri is a personal tracking and awareness tool only. It does not diagnose, treat, or replace advice from a qualified healthcare professional. This privacy policy covers data handling only — for the app's full terms of use, see the Terms of Service inside the app.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top of this page. Continued use of the app after changes means you accept the updated policy.
